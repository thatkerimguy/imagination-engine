# 10. World and spaces

Some novels are places to explore: a house, a ship, a town. The **World** workspace lets you lay
the place out as rooms on a map, join them with exits, and give the reader a map they can open
beside the story. You can still use every block inside a room: a room is just a passage that
knows where it is.

## Rooms

Open **World** from the left rail (on the phone: **More → World**). The **Map** tab shows a grid.

1. **Tap an empty square** to make your first room. The Studio makes a passage for it (named
   after the room) that already ends with an **Exits** block.
2. **Select the room**, then tap a square **next to it**. The new room is joined to the
   selected one both ways: if it sits to the east, the first room gets an exit **East** and the
   new one an exit **West**. Squares that will join this way show a stronger outline.
3. **Tap any room** to edit it on the right (below the map on a phone):
   - **Name** in English and Turkish. This is what readers see on exits and on the map.
   - **Passage**: the passage that plays when the reader walks in. **Open in Write** takes you
     there. Any way into that passage (a choice, a Go to, loading a save) also counts as being
     in the room.
   - **Across / Down**: the square on the map. You can also move a room by changing these.
   - **Secret**: the room stays off the map until the reader has been there.

Deleting a room removes the exits that led into it. Its passage stays in Write.

## Exits

Each room lists its **Exits**. An exit has:

- a **Direction**: north, northeast, east… or **Up**, **Down**, **In**, **Out**, for stairs and
  doors that don't follow the grid (the map draws those as dashed lines);
- where it **Leads to**;
- behind the sliders button: **words instead of the direction** ("Through the hatch"), **Show only
  if**, **Can be used only if**, and the **reason shown while locked**. These work exactly
  like a choice option's conditions (chapter 2).

To add an exit, pick the room it leads to and press **Add exit**. The direction follows the map
when the two rooms touch. Keep **Two-way** on to add the way back as well.

## The Exits block

The reader only gets to choose a way out when the room's passage reaches an **Exits** block. Put
it at the end of the passage, after the room's description. It shows each exit as a choice
("East · Garden"), hides or locks exits by their conditions, and walks the reader there when
they tap one. In Write, the block names its room and offers an optional question ("Where to?").

If a room's passage has no Exits block, its card in World says so and offers **Add an Exits
block to its passage**. An Exits block in a passage that isn't a room gets a warning in
Problems: it has nothing to offer.

## What the map shows

Above the grid:

- **The map shows**: only rooms the reader has visited; visited rooms **and where their exits
  lead** (shown as "Unexplored", the default); or every room that isn't secret.
- **Fast travel**: at an Exits block, the reader can also tap any room they have visited on the
  map, not only the ones next door.

## Spaces

**Spaces** are views beside the story. On a phone the reader swipes left or right to reach them;
on a computer they click the arrow at the edge of the screen, and **Esc** brings them back to
the story. While a space is open, the story waits: auto-advance and choice timers hold.

Open the **Spaces** tab in World:

- **Add a map space**: the map of your rooms. The reader can tap a room on it to walk there
  (when it's next door, or with fast travel), and the story comes back at once.
- **Add a screen space**: one of your **panel screens** from Design (chapter 7), such as a
  journal or stats page, always one swipe away.

Each space has a **name** (shown at its top and on the edge arrow), a **side** (left or right of
the story) and what it **shows**. The arrows change the order; the line at the top shows the
reading order from left to right, for example _Map · Story · Notes_. A novel can have up to six
spaces.

## Try it

1. In World, make a **Hall**, then a **Garden** east of it.
2. On the Spaces tab, **Add a map space**.
3. Back on the Map tab, select Hall, **Open in Write**, write a sentence above the Exits block,
   and press **Play from here**.
4. Tap **East · Garden**. Then swipe right (or click the left edge) to see the map, and tap
   **Hall** to walk back.
